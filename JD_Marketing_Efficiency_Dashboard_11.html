Data notes. Weeks are Sun–Sat. W24 = 12 to 18 Jul 2026 (current), W23 = 5 to 11 Jul 2026 (prior), with W20 to W22 retained for trend depth. Shopify site metrics now come from the RC eCommerce KPIs export (net sales, reconciles exactly with the weekly summary); the Marketing Attribution export shipped at Month granularity this cycle, so the traffic-type table is monthly (Jun vs May) rather than weekly. First-click attribution. GA4 tables use gross revenue and GA4 channel/campaign groupings (primary). SHOPIFY sections use net sales and are labelled with the blue tag (secondary). New Order Share = new orders ÷ total orders for that row; New Rev Share = new gross revenue ÷ total revenue for that row; both shares show a percentage-point (pts) change week on week. Two ROAS views where available: Platform ROAS = ad-platform conversion value ÷ spend; Shopify-attributed New ROAS = GA4 new gross revenue ÷ spend. Meta conversion value is derived per row as spend × ROAS (raw export ships a cumulative column). The Overview overall-performance chart is the Shopify daily business feed (net sales, spend, MER), aggregated to the selected granularity. Monthly Dec and Jun are partial.
